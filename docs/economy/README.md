# Economy and progression simulation

Generated by `pnpm --filter @nyrgo/sim sim` (100 players per archetype, 90 days, seed `nyrgo`). Do not edit by hand: change the model or the values and run it again.

Every simulated player plays on the game's own rules from `@nyrgo/shared`: the battle engine, capture chances, place loot, daily missions, level curves, the daily gold cap and evolution conditions. Only the choices are simulated, with a simple sensible policy: keep a stock of vaults and patches, fight with a strong-enough creature (training the one closest to evolving), use the best vault on rare finds, evolve as soon as it can afford it, craft only with spare materials.

## Changes from v1 to v2

| Value | v1 | v2 | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `spawns.stageWeights` | all equal | {"1":85,"2":13,"3":2} | Among creatures of the same rarity, evolved forms were as likely as first forms; now they are the exception. (Together with the level limit below.) |
| `spawns.wildLevelAbovePlayer` | null | 3 | Evolved wild creatures come at levels 10–35, so a new player could catch a level-30 creature on day one. Now each player meets a wild creature at most 3 levels above their own: catching does not skip progression and fights stay fair. |
| `spawns.rarityWeights` | {"common":62,"uncommon":25,"rare":10,"epic":3} | {"common":64,"uncommon":26,"rare":8,"epic":2} | Rare and epic finds a little rarer, so the Dex stays a goal past the first month. |
| `capture.baseChance (rare, epic)` | [0.25,0.12] | [0.2,0.09] | Rare and epic creatures were caught in under two throws on average. |
| `economy.dailyGameplayGoldCap` | 1500 | 500 | With the lower rewards nobody reached 1,500 a day, so the cap protected nothing; at 500 it only stops long farming sessions (and bots), never a regular player. |
| `rewards.levelUpGoldPerLevel` | 20 | 5 | Level-up gold grew with the level (20 × L) and was the largest single source of gold. |
| `rewards.dailyMission (xp, gold)` | [500,150] | [150,60] | Missions alone gave 1,500 XP a day (the regular player reached level 30 in under a month) and were the second largest source of gold. |
| `rewards.captureSuccess (xp, gold)` | [100,10] | [40,5] | XP and gold per catch, with the rarity multiplier on the XP. |
| `rewards.pveWin (xp, gold)` | [150,25] | [60,12] | XP and gold per rewarded win (still 20 a day). |
| `rewards.poiInteract (xp, gold, items)` | [25,"20–40","2–4"] | [15,"8–16","1–3"] | Places are visited often: their gold added up fastest for long sessions, and they gave more vaults than players could throw, so nobody needed the shop. |
| `economy.poiLoot (basic vault, each material)` | [50,3] | [30,6] | Fewer free vaults so the shop matters; twice the evolution materials so evolving is not stuck waiting for drops. |
| `rewards.newCell.gold` | 5 | 3 | Not implemented in the API yet; lowered so the capped daily rewards (battles, missions, new cells) still fit under the new 500 cap. |
| `economy.creatureBattleXp` | {"base":20,"perFoeLevel":6} | {"base":80,"perFoeLevel":20} | A creature needed ~65 wins to reach its evolution level, so level evolutions almost never happened; about 3.5 times the XP brings the first one into the first two weeks. |

## Assumptions

| Archetype | Days a week | Minutes a day | Throw skill | Fights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Casual | 3 | 20 | 0.35 | no |
| Habitual | 6 | 45 | 0.5 | yes |
| Dedicado | 7 | 90 | 0.6 | yes |
| Intensivo | 7 | 180 | 0.7 | yes |

- 10 reachable wild creatures and 6 place visits per hour of walking (central Córdoba; derivation in `packages/sim/src/model.ts`).
- Spawn cells along a route: urban 45 %, historic 30 %, park 15 %, culture 10 %.
- Creatures rest fully overnight and recover 10 % of their HP every 5 minutes of walking.
- Commons the player already has four of are left alone; everything else is caught.
- Not modelled: trading, events with legendary or mythic creatures, lures, NEAR claims (Phase 3+), the first-cell exploration reward (configured but not implemented in the API).
